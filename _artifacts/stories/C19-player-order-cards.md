## Story

As the designer,
I want the six player-order cards (1–6) generated as a printable two-sided deck
from the approved mockup,
so that seating/turn order is dealt from a physical deck and the cards join the
shared card-print set.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Six cards, 1–6**, generated by `tools/gen-player-order.mjs` (real generator,
   promoted from `tools/mockup-player-order.mjs`).
2. **Landscape 67×44mm — the same physical card as every other deck** (designer,
   2026-08-17: "in landscape just like every other card; it can rotate the text").
   Uniform card size means it pools onto the shared sheets.
3. **The number is the card, rotated 90°** — Leftfield Serif ~300pt, ink ≈ 50mm; the
   digit is turned a quarter-turn (you turn the card to read it), which lets it span
   the long 67mm axis so it stays huge. Centering carries the designer's **verified
   upright nudge**: `translateY(-9.4mm)` moves the ink to the box centre, applied
   *first*, then rotate about the element centre — `rotate(90deg) translateY(-9.4mm)`
   — so the ink stays centred on the corrected axis. The side bearing is left on the
   box (uncorrected), now on the vertical axis.
4. **One uniform back on all six** (18Dragon / "Player Order" + rule lines, horizontal
   per the mockup), so a face-down row deals out genuinely random.
5. **Two-sided duplex print**, same recipe as the other decks: crop-marks-only (no
   card border), `print-color-adjust:exact`. Front = the six numbers; back = uniform
   backs, registered for the long-edge flip.
6. **Joins the shared print** — pooled by `gen-cards.mjs` onto the **duplex** sheets
   at the tail, filling the last page's otherwise-blank slots (trains 83 + privates
   30 + player-order 6 = 119 → still 10 pages, no extra sheet). A standalone
   `gen-player-order.mjs` also emits `print/cards-player-order.html` for isolated
   preview.
7. **Renders verified** at true size — the six faces centred (rotated), the uniform
   back, safe crop margins, and front/back registration on the pooled last page.

## Dev Notes

**Design is locked by the mockup** (`docs/mockups/player-order-mockup.html`, approved
2026-08-17). Two alternates were mocked and dropped (a foot caption; the number
repeated upside-down for the opposite player) — the plain number won.

**"Reference cards" dropped** (designer, 2026-08-17): the old C19 title said
"(player-order, reference)"; there is no reference deck — that was an unneeded
assumption. C19 is the player-order deck only.

**Design pivot (designer, 2026-08-17):** the mockup was portrait 44×67 ("the only
portrait card, identifiable by orientation"). The designer changed it to **landscape
67×44 like every other card** ("it can rotate the text as long as the positioning is
accurate") — uniform card size wins, and it now **pools onto the shared duplex
sheets** instead of getting its own. The number is rotated 90°; you turn the card to
read it (which also keeps the digit spanning the long axis, so it stays huge).

**Centering after rotation:** the designer's verified upright nudge is
`translateY(-9.4mm)` (moves the ink to its line-box centre; the side bearing is left
on the box). Applying that *first* and then rotating about the element centre —
`rotate(90deg) translateY(-9.4mm)` — keeps the ink centred on the corrected axis by
construction (rotating a centred ink about the centre preserves it). Re-measure the
9.4mm if the point size changes.

### Completion Notes

- **Landscape 67×44, number rotated 90°** (designer pivot from the portrait mockup):
  the player-order card is now the same physical card as every deck, so it pools onto
  the shared print. Turning the card to read the number keeps the digit spanning the
  long 67mm axis (~50mm ink).
- **Centring is accurate by construction:** `rotate(90deg) translateY(-9.4mm)` — the
  verified upright nudge moves the ink to the box centre first, then the whole glyph
  rotates about that centre, so the ink stays centred on the corrected axis. All six
  digits share the transform → identical horizontal centring; the small vertical
  differences are the uncorrected side bearing (the "leave it on the box" choice).
- **Pooled into the duplex print** (`gen-cards.mjs`): the 6 cards append to the
  trains+privates pool at the tail, filling the last page's blanks — trains 83 +
  privates 30 + player-order 6 = **119 → still 10 pages / 20 duplex sheets**, no extra
  sheet. Verified: the pooled last-page front (5 privates + 6 numbers) and its
  rows-mirrored back register exactly (uniform backs behind the numbers; P+ / private
  backs correct).
- **Uniform back** reverted to the mockup's **horizontal** layout (it's cosmetic;
  rotating the whole back card risked a portrait footprint in the grid).
- **Bleed-safe gradient** (designer, 2026-08-17): the paper gradient was directional
  (light top → warm bottom), so vertically-abutting cards met at a colour seam and a
  slightly-off cut left a mismatched strip. Made it **vertically symmetric** (warm at
  both top and bottom edges, light only in the interior) so every card edge bleeds
  into a matching tone. (Horizontal edges already matched — a vertical gradient is
  uniform across x.)
- **Standalone** `gen-player-order.mjs` still emits `print/cards-player-order.html`
  (landscape 3×2, front + uniform back) for isolated preview.
