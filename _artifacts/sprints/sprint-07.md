**Why this alone.** A small, focused polish sprint to apply the adjustments the designer spotted once
the whole map rendered together — before opening the privates/companies track. Keeps the board changes
isolated and reviewable.

**Scope (from C41, designer-confirmed):**
- **Verantum:** remove rows C & D entirely + reshape the sea (row E becomes the new north coast); add a
  north-coast city + adjacent dit; add a **gray city at J3** (size 2, track sides {3,4}→I4/J5, values
  30/40/50/60); **relocate minor home 3** from Verantia (H9) to the J3 city (Verantia keeps 1,2).
- **Caelimor:** add a **special-tile city at J27** + a **dit at K26** (its SW).
- Homes stay **1–30** (3 relocated, not renumbered).

**Deferred / out of scope:** the privates/companies track (designer drafting `author-privates.md`) — a
later sprint. No other regions touched.

**Open items to confirm at build** (per C41): the north-coast city hex + its dit hex; new names
(Latin/Roman for Verantum, Celtic-ish for Caelimor) + J27's special letter; the exact sea reshape; the
gray city's revenue-ladder rendering. (J3 + track sides {3,4} already settled.)
