# 18Dragon — World Bible

The fiction of 18Dragon: the five regions, their peoples, capitals, and the
naming conventions that keep each culture's voice distinct. This is deliverable
worldbuilding — the game's *mechanical* structure lives in
`_artifacts/prd-game.md § 3`, which points here for flavor.

> Railways in a Fantasy Land.

---

## The Five Regions

### A — Verantum (the island)
- **Color:** Purple `#7B2D8B` · **Terrain:** Ruins · **Capital:** Verantia
- An ancient fallen empire, now a ruined island. Its derelict cities are shadows
  of former glory — worth little today, but capable of being rebuilt into the
  richest destinations on the map.
- **Culture:** Latin / Roman.

### N — Caelimor (the northwest)
- **Color:** Sky blue `#56B4E9` · **Terrain:** Coastal / plains · **Capital:** Draeven
- A maritime realm of coasts and open plains.
- **Culture:** Celtic-ish.

### D — Gördum (the center-north)
- **Color:** Amber `#E69F00` · **Terrain:** Mountains · **Capital:** Brekheim
- A dwarven kingdom in the mountains — the people who **invented the trains**.
- **Culture:** Germanic / Norse.

### E — Varstova (the northeast)
- **Color:** Teal `#009E73` · **Terrain:** Hills · **Capital:** Varstgrad
- A mercantile land of hills.
- **Culture:** Slavic-ish.

### S — Muravel (the south)
- **Color:** Vermillion `#D55E00` · **Terrain:** Plains · **Capital:** Kalavar
- A wide southern trading zone, Mediterranean in feel.
- **Culture:** Mediterranean / Arabic.

Region colors are from the Okabe-Ito colorblind-safe palette (plus purple for
Verantum).

## Naming Conventions (phonetics)

Each region has a distinct phonetic signature. New place, company, and character
names should honor the region's sound so the world stays coherent.

| Region | Feel | Signature |
|--------|------|-----------|
| **Verantum** | Latin / Roman | hard C/V/L/R; endings in *-um, -ia, -ium, -antia* |
| **Caelimor** | Celtic-ish | *ae/ei* digraphs; endings in *-mor, -dun, -ael* |
| **Gördum** | Germanic / Norse | hard K/G/D/R/B; compounds; endings in *-dum, -rok, -geld* |
| **Varstova** | Slavic-ish | endings in *-ov, -esh, -var, -ova* |
| **Muravel** | Mediterranean / Arabic | open vowels; optional *al-* prefix; endings in *-vel, -sal, -eem* |
